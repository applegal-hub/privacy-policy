Privacy Policy · ZUP

Effective date: 20 August 2026 iOS

This Privacy Policy explains how we handles information when you use the ZUP mobile application (the “App”). ZUP shows foreign-exchange and precious-metal quotes, charts, headlines, and related tools. It is a reference tool only and is not a brokerage or investment service.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the iOS version of ZUP. It does not cover third-party websites or apps you open from ZUP (for example, when you tap “Read article” and Safari opens). Those services have their own privacy policies.

2. We do not require an account

ZUP version 1.0 does not provide user registration, login, or profile accounts. We do not ask you to give us your name, email address, phone number, or payment card details to use the App.

3. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following on your device (for example in local storage), so your preferences survive app restarts:

  • Watchlist and quote display preferences
  • Price-alert settings
  • Saved news items and browsing history
  • Economic-calendar follows and in-app messages
  • Language and theme
  • Cached quotes and news for offline display
  • Optional in-app feedback text (kept on the device; it is not uploaded by the current version)

You can remove this information by using in-app “Clear cache” where available, or by deleting the App.

4. Information processed when the App is online

To show quotes, charts, news, and the calendar, the App sends ordinary network requests to public or third-party services. Those requests are needed to download market data and headlines. They typically include:

  • Technical data such as your IP address, device type, and request time (as seen by the destination server)
  • The market symbol or search query needed to fetch a rate, chart, or headline

We do not use this to build an advertising profile. We do not sell your information.

Depending on the feature, the App may contact providers such as:

  • Public FX reference sources (including ECB-related public APIs such as Frankfurter)
  • Public precious-metal and market-data endpoints (for example gold-price APIs, Yahoo Finance chart data, and similar public market APIs)
  • News feeds, including Google News RSS (via a public RSS converter when needed) and market-headline APIs
  • Public economic-calendar JSON feeds
  • Our own configuration server, used only to load App settings

Those providers process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control how they log IP addresses or other server data.

5. Links opened outside the App

Original news articles open in your system browser (Safari on iPhone). After you leave ZUP, that website’s privacy policy applies.

6. Permissions and identifiers

  • The App does not use IDFA / App Tracking Transparency advertising tracking.
  • The App does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location for its core features.
  • We do not use the App for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • If iOS shows a network or local-network related prompt, it is only so the App can load quotes and news.

7. Children

ZUP is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will delete it where we hold it.

8. How we use information

On-device data is used only to:

  • Remember your watchlist, alerts, language, and theme
  • Show cached content when the network fails
  • Provide in-app reminders and messages you opted into

Network requests are used only to:

  • Display quotes, charts, news headlines, and calendar events
  • Apply App configuration

We do not use your information to:

  • Sell or rent mailing lists
  • Serve third-party ads
  • Make automated credit or hiring decisions

9. Legal bases (if you are in the EEA/UK)

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies, we process information on these bases:

  • Performance of a contract / requested service: fetching the quotes and headlines you ask the App to show
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the App secure and operating, and storing preferences on your device
  • Consent: where required by law for optional items

You may object to processing or request access, correction, deletion, or restriction as provided by law. Because most data stays on your device, the fastest way to delete it is to clear App data or uninstall ZUP.

10. International transfers

Market-data and news servers may be located outside your country (including the United States and the European Union). If you use the App, your device will contact those servers. Their processing is governed by their policies.

11. Data retention

  • On-device data remains until you clear it or delete the App.
  • We do not operate a user-account database for ZUP 1.0.
  • Server logs on our configuration server or on third-party APIs, if any, are retained only as those operators require for security and operations.

12. Security

We use standard HTTPS where the destination supports it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Do not submit secrets (passwords, bank details) in in-app feedback.

13. Your choices

  • Turn off cellular/Wi-Fi to stop new network requests (cached data may still show).
  • Clear cache or delete the App to remove local data.
  • Use iOS Settings to control Safari and tracking at the system level.
  • Contact us to ask how this policy applies.

14. Not financial advice

Quotes and news in ZUP are for general information only. They may be delayed or incomplete and are not investment, tax, or trading advice.

15. Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes. The new effective date will appear at the top. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
