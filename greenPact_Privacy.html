Privacy Policy

Effective: August 15, 2026 · Last updated: August 22, 2026
GreenPact Volunteer (also referred to as “GreenPact,” “we,” “us,” or “the app”) is a United States mobile application for discovering public nature volunteer activities, logging personal low-carbon daily actions, and reading nature education and NGO news.
1. Who this app is for

GreenPact Volunteer is intended for users in the United States who are 13 years of age or older. Some volunteer events require volunteers to be 16+ or 18+, as stated by the event organizer. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

2. Information we process

Account information: display name, email address, and password you enter when you register or sign in. Passwords are stored only in local device storage for this prototype build.

Volunteer applications: name, email, phone number, ZIP code, emergency contact, optional experience notes, activity identifier, application status (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Completed, Missed), and timestamps.

Check-in photos: photos you take with the device camera for daily check-ins and for on-site volunteer check-in. The app uses the camera only; it does not access your photo library. Photos are stored on your device as part of your check-in record.

Location (optional): if you tap “Use my location,” the app requests location while you use the app so it can estimate distance to volunteer sites. You can decline location access and search by 5-digit US ZIP code instead. Location is used on-device for distance display. We do not sell location data.

Usage preferences: language (English or Chinese), notification preference, points, badges, and saved articles.

Device permissions: Location (when in use) and Camera. You can refuse or later revoke these in iOS or Android Settings.

3. How we use information

We use the information above solely to:

  • create and keep your on-device account session;
  • submit and show volunteer applications and their status;
  • record daily and on-site check-ins, points, and badges;
  • show nearby activities when you share location or a ZIP code;
  • remember articles you save and your language/notification settings; and
  • honor your request to delete your account and associated data.
We do not use your information for advertising, profiling for ads, or selling data.
4. Payments and donations

GreenPact Volunteer does not sell products, does not process payments, and does not collect donations, tips, or in-app purchases. If an organizer publishes a donation webpage, the app opens it in Safari or your default browser. That website is not part of GreenPact Volunteer and has its own privacy policy. We do not receive payment card numbers.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers. We do not operate a backend that uploads your account data in this version. If you apply to a volunteer event, the organizer named in the activity listing is independently responsible for any records they keep about attendance.

6. Retention

Information remains on your device until you sign out, delete the app, or use Delete account. Signing out ends the current session but does not erase that email’s locally stored history. Deleting the account removes the profile and associated personal data stored by the app for that email on this device.

7. Your privacy rights (including CCPA)

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). To delete personal information the app stores about you: sign in, then use Delete account and confirm your email. We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

California residents: delete your account and personal data in the app Profile → Delete account
8. Security

This prototype stores data in the operating system’s local app storage. Protect your device with a passcode and do not share your GreenPact Volunteer password.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
